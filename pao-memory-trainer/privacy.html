Privacy Policy

PAO Memory Trainer

Last updated: August 25, 2026

PAO Memory Trainer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind.

All information you enter into the app (your Person-Action-Object associations for playing cards and numbers) is stored only in local storage on your own device. It is never sent to any server, third party, or the developer, because the app has no network access and no backend — it works entirely offline.

The app requests no special permissions, contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking libraries.

If you uninstall the app, all data stored by it is deleted along with it.

This policy may be updated if a future version of the app changes what data, if any, it collects. Any such change will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above before that version is released.
